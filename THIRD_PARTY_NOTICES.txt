This package contains project code copied from the existing LF search and
the immuno_mpnn_prior model/data helpers, plus the original project MPNN
checkpoint. Source identities and checksums are in SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json.
No new license is asserted for those original components or model weights;
retain their original terms when redistributing beyond the intended project.

The RFProteina AF3 wrapper files are copied from rfd4-proteina/v0.0.2.
Its BSD 3-Clause notice is retained at vendor/af3/LICENSE.
vendor/af3/af3_runtime.py retains its original DeepMind copyright and
Apache-2.0 header and attribution to the upstream inference implementation.

NetMHCpan, AF3 parameters, and cluster container images are external
dependencies and are not redistributed in this package. Use installations
and parameter access available to your own cluster account.

The external AlphaFold 3 engine used by the compatible image is
kierandidi/alphafold3 commit 8d3601151d5bf7525d8591583915d2a8471442a5.
Its source is not bundled. Exact engine and container-build provenance are in
runtime/af3/PROVENANCE.json. The inspected container recipe is included; the
container is not. The Apache-2.0 text for the included inference adaptation is
retained at vendor/af3/Apache-2.0.txt. External engine, model parameters, and
outputs retain their original terms; see the linked upstream instructions.

The readability edits and new preparation, UI, testing, and packaging helpers
do not replace the original component licenses or assert a blanket license
over the package. Gradio and other installed Python dependencies retain their
own licenses and are installed separately.
